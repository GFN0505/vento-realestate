# Set up your organization

Create your company's Vento account and its first company (entity). It takes about two minutes.

1. Go to **Create a company account** and enter your name, company, work email and a password. The password needs at least 12 characters and a number, and can't be a common or leaked password. The rules tick as you type.
2. Open the email from Vento and click the link, or type the 6-digit code. Codes last 30 minutes; you can ask for a new one after a minute.
3. **Your organization:** the developer group as a whole. Check the name, choose your head office state and when your financial year starts.
4. **First entity:** the company that owns your first project, often an SPV. Add its RC number and TIN now or later, and say whether it's VAT registered.
5. As the owner, you set up two-step sign-in next (see *Sign in with two-step*).

Leave half-way? Sign in again and Vento takes you back to the step you were on.
