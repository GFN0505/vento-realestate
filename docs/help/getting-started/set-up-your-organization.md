# Set up your organization

Create your company's Vento Real Estate account and its first company. It takes about two minutes.

1. Go to **Create a company account** and enter your name, company, work email and a password. The password needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character such as ! or #. The rules tick as you type. Type it again in **Confirm password**.
2. Open the email from Vento Real Estate and click the link, or type the 6-digit code. Codes last 30 minutes; you can ask for a new one after a minute.
3. **Your organization:** the developer group as a whole. Check the name, choose your head office state and when your financial year starts.
4. **First company:** the company that owns your first project, often an SPV. Add its RC number and TIN now or later, and say whether it's VAT registered.
5. As the owner, you set up two-step sign-in next (see *Sign in with two-step*).

Leave half-way? Sign in again and Vento Real Estate takes you back to the step you were on.
