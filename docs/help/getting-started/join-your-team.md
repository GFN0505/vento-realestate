# Join your team on Vento

Someone at your company invited you. The invitation email shows the company, who invited you, your role and which companies you can see. Invitations last 7 days.

1. Click **Accept invitation** in the email.
2. Check the details: company, role, what your role can approve, and whether two-step sign-in is required.
3. **New to Vento?** Enter your name and choose a password (at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character), then type it again in **Confirm password**. **Already use Vento?** Sign in with your existing account instead, and you'll belong to both companies.
4. If your role needs two-step sign-in, set it up now (see *Sign in with two-step*).

**If the link doesn't work**
- *"This invitation expired"*: ask the person named to send a new one.
- *"This invitation was withdrawn"*: it was cancelled; ask your company if you should still join.
- *"This invitation is for another email"*: you're signed in as someone else. Choose **Sign out and continue**.
