# Sign in with two-step

Owners, admins, finance and HR must use two-step sign-in, and your organization may require it for everyone. Anyone else can turn it on in **My security**.

**Set it up (once)**
1. Confirm your password.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or similar), or type the key shown.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from the app.
4. Save your 10 backup codes (Download or Copy) and tick **I've saved my backup codes**. Each code works once.

**Sign in with a passkey (fastest)**
If you've added a passkey (see *Keep your account secure*), click the email field and pick your passkey, or click **Sign in with a passkey**, then use your face, fingerprint or device PIN. No code is needed.

**Every sign-in with a password**
1. Enter your work email and password. Tick **Keep me signed in** only on your own device: it lasts 30 days. Otherwise you're signed out after 30 minutes without activity, or 12 hours at most.
2. Enter the newest 6-digit code from your app, or choose **Use a backup code**.

**If something goes wrong**
- *"Invalid email or password"*: check both, or use **Forgot password?**
- *Too many wrong tries*: sign-in pauses for 15 minutes and we email you. Wait for the countdown, or reset your password.
- *Lost your phone and your backup codes*: ask an owner or admin to reset your two-step. It also removes your passkeys. You'll get an email to set a new password, then you set up two-step again.
- *Lost a phone that's still signed in*: from another device, open **My security** and choose **Sign out** next to it.
- *Forgot your password*: choose **Forgot password?**, open the link (valid 30 minutes) and choose a new password that follows the same rules as when you signed up and isn't one of your last 5. Type it twice to confirm. You're signed out of every other device.
