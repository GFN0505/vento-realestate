# Set up your organization and companies

Owners and admins with access to every company manage the organization's details and its companies in **Settings**.

**Organization details**
Go to **Settings → Organization**. Correct the name, head office state or the month your financial year starts, then click **Save changes**.

**Add a company**
A company is a separate registered company, like the SPV you set up for one estate. To add another estate under a company you already have, create a project instead (coming with Projects).

1. Click **Add company**: on **Settings → Companies**, at the bottom of the **Company** menu at the top of the page, or on the setup checklist on Home.
2. Enter the name and choose **Holding company** or **Project company (SPV)**.
3. Add the RC number and TIN now or later, and say whether it's VAT registered.
4. Click **Add company**.

Each RC number can belong to only one company. If an archived company already has it, choose **Restore it instead**.

**Archive a company that's wound up**
On its row, open ⋯ and choose **Archive**. **Undo** in the message brings it back. If someone can only see that company, Vento names them first. Archiving your holding company always asks first. You can't archive your last active company.

**Bring one back**
Choose **Archived** above the list, then **Restore** on its row. It counts towards your plan again.

**If your plan is full**
Owners see how many companies their plan allows. Archive one you no longer use, or **Talk to Vento** about a bigger plan. Admins see which owner to ask.
