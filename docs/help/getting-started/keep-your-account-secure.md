# Keep your account secure

Everyone has a **My security** page. Open it from your name at the bottom of the side panel, or press ⌘K and type "security".

**Turn on two-step sign-in**
In the *Sign-in* card, click **Turn on**, confirm your password, scan the QR code with an authenticator app, enter the 6-digit code and save your backup codes. If your role or your organization requires two-step, it's already on and can't be turned off.

**Backup codes**
The card shows how many are left. **Make new codes** replaces them all; save the new ones somewhere safe.

**Change your password**
Click **Change password**, enter your current password and the new one twice.

**Add a passkey**
A passkey lets you sign in with your face, fingerprint or device PIN instead of a password and code. In the *Passkeys* card, click **Add a passkey** and follow your device. Only add one on a device that's yours. You can rename or remove a passkey from its ⋯ menu. You can have up to 10.

**Where you're signed in**
The last card lists every device signed in to your account, with its browser, approximate place and when it signed in. **Sign out** ends one; **Sign out other devices** ends all except this one. Use it if you lose a phone.

**Emails we send**
We email you when your account is used from a new browser, when a passkey is added or removed, when two-step is turned off and when your password changes. If it wasn't you, change your password and sign out other devices.

**For owners: require two-step for everyone**
In **Settings → Organization → Security**, turn on **Require two-step for everyone**. People who don't have it see a banner and have 7 days to set it up. Turning it off emails every owner.
