# Manage your team

Owners and admins manage people in **Settings → Team**. The list shows everyone in the organization first, owners at the top, then the people you've invited. Changes take effect straight away, and every change is recorded.

**Find someone**
Search by name or email, or filter by role. Choose **Invited** or **Removed** above the list to see only those people. If you pick one company at the top of the page, the list shows only the people who can work in that company. Click **Show all entities** to see everyone again.

**Change someone's role**
Click the role on their row and choose a new one. The message that appears has **Undo**. Making someone an **Owner** asks you to confirm first, and every owner gets an email about it.

**Limit someone to some companies**
Click their row, choose **Only some entities**, tick the companies, then click **Save changes**.

**They lost their phone**
On their row, open ⋯ and choose **Reset two-step**. They get an email to set a new password, then set up two-step again the next time they sign in.

**Someone has left**
On their row, open ⋯ and choose **Remove from team**. Their access ends at once, and everything they did stays on record. To bring them back later, choose **Removed** above the list and click **Invite again**.

**Things you can't change**
- Your own access: ask another owner.
- An owner's access, if you're an admin: only owners can change it.
- People with access to companies outside your own, if your access is limited.
