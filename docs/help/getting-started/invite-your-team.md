# Invite your team

Owners and admins can invite people to the organization. Each person gets an email with a link that lasts 7 days.

1. Go to **Settings → Team** and click **Invite people**. You can also click **Invite people** on the setup checklist on Home, or press ⌘K and type "Invite".
2. Paste or type up to 20 email addresses. A list copied from Excel, Gmail or Outlook works. Duplicates are merged, and a likely typo shows "Did you mean…?".
3. Check the **Role**. Vento Real Estate picks the one you used last. To give people different roles, click **Give people different roles** and choose a role on each row.
4. Check **Access**. Everyone can work in all your companies unless you click **Change** and choose **Only some companies**. If you picked one company at the top of the page, the invitation starts with just that company.
5. Click **Send**. To see who you invited, click **View invited** in the message that appears, or choose **Invited** above the list.

**If a row is marked**
- *Already on the team* or *Already invited*: nothing is sent to that address. For someone already invited, choose **Resend instead**.
- *Check this email*: fix the address, or click **Remove**.
- Marked rows don't stop the others: **Send** sends only the unmarked ones and keeps the marked rows so you can fix them.

**If your plan is full**
Inviting someone as an owner needs approval by another owner first, with the same **Why?** question. The invitation is sent once it's approved. Resending an owner's invitation needs approval too.

Owners see how many places are left, for example "You have 1 place left on your Starter plan." Choose **Send only 1**, or **Talk to Vento Real Estate** about a bigger plan. Admins see which owner to ask.

**Later**
On an invitation's ⋯ menu, **Resend** sends a fresh link that lasts another 7 days. **Withdraw** cancels it, and **Undo** in the message brings it back. "Email not delivered" means the address bounced: check it and invite again.
