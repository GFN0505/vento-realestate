# Approve or reject a request

Some changes in Vento Real Estate need a second person before they happen. Until it's approved, nothing changes. Nobody can approve their own request.

**When something is waiting for you**
You get an "Approval needed" email, and **Approvals** at the top of the page shows a count. Click either one. Approving always happens in Vento Real Estate, never from the email.

**Approve**
1. Open **Approvals**. The oldest request waiting for you is already open.
2. Read what changes (before and after), the reason the person gave, and who has approved so far.
3. Click the green button, for example **Approve role change**. The button says what will happen, so there is no second question.

The change is made as soon as the last person approves, and the person who asked gets an email. The next request opens for you.

**Reject**
Type a comment (it's required), then click **Reject**. Nothing changes, and the person sees your comment.

**Keep track of your own requests**
- **Waiting for me** shows what needs you.
- **My requests** shows what you asked for and where it stands. Click **Cancel request** while it's still waiting.
- **Decided** shows what you approved or rejected.

On a phone, tap a request to open it. The buttons stay at the bottom of the screen.

**Good to know**
- A request expires after 7 days without a decision. Ask again if it's still needed.
- If the record changed after it was asked (for example, someone else edited the role), the request expires and you'll see why.
- If two people approve at the same moment, the second sees "… approved this a moment ago."
- If you asked for it, you can't approve it. You'll see "This is your own request."
- Approvers need two-step sign-in. If your role can approve, you'll be asked to set it up (you get 7 days).
- You need a connection. When you're offline the buttons are off.

**Keyboard**
**J** and **K** move through the list, **A** approves, and **R** goes to the comment box.
