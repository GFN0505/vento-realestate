# Help centre

The in-app help centre and contextual help, written for customers (not developers). Articles live here as Markdown and are published into the app's help panel.

## Rules
- Every shipped journey (`docs/journeys/`) gets an article here in the same change that ships it. The article follows the journey's steps in the customer's words, with screenshots taken from the seed data.
- Follow the `ux-writing` skill: plain English, domain terms, no internal jargon, no field names.
- One task per article. Title starts with a verb: "Record a receipt", "Reschedule a payment plan".
- Each article ends with "Related" links to the next likely task.

## Structure
```
docs/help/
  getting-started/   sign in, set up your organization, invite your team, import from Excel
  land/              parcels, title stages, encumbrances
  projects/          projects, BoQ, site diary, purchase orders
  sales/             units and prices, leads, subscriptions, reminders, cancellations, handover
  finance/           receipts, Paystack, bank matching, foreign currency, WHT, period close
  hr/                payroll, site workers, leave
  estate/            service charge, maintenance
  compliance/        KYC, AML reviews, permits
  documents/         templates, contracts, e-signature
  buyers/            articles for buyers, shown in the buyer portal
  realtors/          articles for realtors, shown in the realtor portal
  ai/                turning AI on, budgets, each AI feature
```
