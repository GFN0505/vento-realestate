# Add a parcel

A parcel is a piece of land your company owns, is buying or is thinking about. It holds the land's title stages, its documents and its history in one place.

**Add a parcel**
1. Open **Land & title** and click **Add parcel**. You can also press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) and choose **New parcel**.
2. Type the parcel's name, for example "Plot 22, Folu Ayo".
3. Choose the state and the LGA, and type the area or town.
4. Enter the size. Pick hectares, square metres or plots. If a plot here isn't 600 m², say how big it is, for example "12 plots of 648 m²".
5. Check the status (it starts on *Under acquisition*) and the company that holds the land.
6. Drop the papers you have into **Documents**, or click to choose them. On a phone you can take a photo instead. Each file starts uploading straight away, and Vento Real Estate guesses what it is from its name. Check the type beside each file and change it if it's wrong.
7. Click **Save parcel**.

The parcel opens with its title stages and its documents.

**Title stages**
Under the documents you'll see the stages this land usually goes through in its state: survey plan, excision gazette and C of O (in the FCT, survey plan, R of O and C of O).
- A stage whose document you added is marked **Granted** when you save. If you typed the document's issue date, the stage gets that date.
- If the land already had a C of O when you bought it, click **Edit stages** and add the deed of assignment and governor's consent.
- If you add a document for a stage that isn't in the list (for example a governor's consent), that stage is added for you.
- Land saved as *Acquired* with a document for every stage opens as **Titled**.

**More options**
Click **More options** to add the acquisition date, what the land cost and who you bought it from. Only people who can edit Land & title or see Finance can see these.

**Good to know**
- Vento Real Estate remembers the state and company you used last on this device, so the next parcel is quicker.
- If another parcel already has the same name, you'll see "There's already a parcel called …" with a link to it. You can still save.
- Files can be PDFs or photos (JPG, PNG or HEIC), up to 25 MB each.
- If your connection drops, the upload pauses and carries on when you're back online, as long as the page stays open. What you typed stays.
- When you're offline, **Save parcel** is off until you're back.
- If you can only work in some companies, you'll only see and choose those.
- To change a parcel later, open it, click **⋯** and choose **Edit parcel**. **Archive** in the same menu takes it off the list at once; click **Undo** if you didn't mean to. An archived parcel keeps its documents and can be restored.
- The map and the parcel's boundary come in a later update.
