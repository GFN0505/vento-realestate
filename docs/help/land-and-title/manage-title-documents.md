# Manage title documents

Every parcel keeps its papers in one place: survey plans, gazettes, C of Os, deeds, family receipts, purchase agreements and letters from the registry. Nothing is ever deleted, and every version is kept.

**Upload a document**
1. Open the parcel and click **Upload document** on the Documents card (or **⋯**, then **Upload document**).
2. Drop the file, choose it, or on a phone take a photo.
3. Check what it is. Vento Real Estate guesses from the file's name. For a stage's document you can add its number and issue date.
4. Click **Upload document**.

If the parcel already has a document of that type, the new file becomes its next version, and the button reads **Upload as v2**. Family receipts, purchase agreements, deeds and other papers can also be kept as separate documents with their own name, for example "Adeyemi family".

**Upload from your phone**
On your Home, tap **Upload document** at the bottom of the screen (you can also find it with ⌘K).
1. The parcel you used last is already chosen.
2. Tap **Take photo** or **Choose file**.
3. Tap **Upload**.

The type starts on the document for the parcel's current stage, unless the file's name says otherwise. Tap **Open parcel** in the message to see it filed.

**Open, download and see earlier versions**
- Click a document's version (for example "v2 · 2 earlier") to see every version with who uploaded it and when.
- **Preview** opens PDFs and photos. On a phone they open in your browser's own viewer. iPhone photos saved as HEIC download instead.
- **Download** saves a copy.

**Remove a version**
If you uploaded the wrong file or put it on the wrong parcel, open the versions and click **Remove**. Say why (for example "Wrong parcel"), then click **Remove version**. It leaves the list straight away; click **Undo** if you change your mind. The file and your reason stay in the parcel's **Activity**.

You can't remove the only file behind a granted stage. Upload the right file first, then remove the wrong one.

**Good to know**
- Files can be PDFs or photos (JPG, PNG or HEIC), up to 25 MB each.
- Uploading a document doesn't change a stage by itself. To mark a stage granted, update the stage.
- If your connection drops, the upload pauses and carries on when you're back online, as long as the page stays open.
- Documents are private. Each preview or download link works for a few minutes only, and only for people who can see the parcel.
