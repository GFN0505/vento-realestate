# Move a title stage

Each parcel shows its title stages in order, for example survey plan, excision gazette, then C of O. When the registry moves, update the stage so everyone sees where the title stands.

**Update a stage**
1. Open the parcel. Click the stage, or the button at the top, for example **Update C of O**. On a phone the button sits at the bottom of the screen.
2. The most likely next status is already chosen, and the date is today. Change them if you need to:
   - **In progress**: you've started work on it.
   - **Submitted**: the application is with the registry.
   - **Queried**: the registry has asked for something.
   - **Granted**: the document has been issued.
   - **Rejected**: the registry refused it.
3. Add the reference (the file number) and who issued it, if you have them.
4. For **Granted**, add the document. If it's already in the parcel's documents, you'll see "Uses C of O v1 from the vault" and you don't need to add it again.
5. Click **Save update**.

The stage moves, and you'll see "C of O granted · Undo". Click **Undo** if you picked the wrong status.

**When you need to say what happened**
- For **Queried**, say what the registry asked for, for example "Asked for the family's consent letter".
- For **Rejected**, say why.
- If you change a stage that was granted, say what happened to it.

**Chasing the registry**
For *Submitted* or *Queried*, add a **Follow up by** date. On that day the stage shows on your Home, so you remember to chase it. A stage with no movement for 90 days also shows there.

**Letters from the registry**
With any status other than *Granted*, a file you add is filed as a letter, for example "Query letter" or "Application acknowledgement". It stays in the parcel's documents under that name, and never replaces the title document itself.

**Resubmit**
For a rejected stage, choose **Submitted** again. The button reads **Resubmit**.

**From Home**
Your Home lists what needs you: queries to answer, follow-ups that are due and stalled stages. Click **Update stage** on one to open that stage. It leaves the list once the stage changes.

**Good to know**
- When every stage is granted on land you've acquired, the parcel becomes **Titled**.
- You can't pick a date after today.
- If a colleague updated the same stage a moment before you, you'll see who. What you typed stays; check theirs, then save again.
- To change which stages a parcel goes through, click **⋯** and choose **Edit stages**. A stage that has started, or that has a document, stays. Every parcel keeps at least one stage.
- Every update is in the parcel's **Activity**.
