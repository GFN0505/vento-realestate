# 0023 · Staff accounts are separate from customer accounts and join by invitation

- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Status:** accepted (owner, 27 Nov 2026, with the admin-console plan, slice 1b). Amends 0015 (who a super-admin is) and 0021 (how a super-admin reaches the console).

## Context
Until now a Vento Real Estate super-admin was any customer account with an extra `platform_admins` row (decision 0015's model). Staff reached the console from a customer workspace, a super-admin could also be an owner or viewer in an organization, and staff power was tangled with customer membership: a customer's two-step reset could touch a super-admin, the console needed a "Back to {organization}" link, and a super-admin with no organization couldn't manage passkeys (a consequence in decision 0021). The owner decided (docs/specs/admin-console.md, slice 1b, 27 Nov 2026) that staff accounts are only for the console, are made only by invitation, and that one email is either a staff or a customer account.

## Decision
- **Two kinds of account.** `users.account_kind` is `customer` or `staff` (migration 0013), set when the account is made and never changed (trigger `users_account_kind_fixed`, migration 0014). Better Auth carries it as a user field (`input: false`), so the session has it with no extra query.
- **Staff join only by invitation.** A staff account is created only by accepting a staff invitation, or the bootstrap invitation on an environment with no super-admin. `staff_invitations` is a platform table (no `organization_id`, decision 0015): a 256-bit token stored as a SHA-256 hash, 48 hours, one pending per email (partial unique index), resend once a minute (new link, fresh 48 hours), withdraw at once, at most 5 open. Expiry is read-time and writes no event.
- **Two-step before power.** The public staff sign-up (`/staff-invitation/{token}`, procedures `console.join.get` and `console.join.start`, rate-limited per network) sets a name and password with no email verification (the invitation proves the inbox) and no Terms; two-step is then set up through Better Auth. Verifying the code makes the person a super-admin and accepts the invitation in one transaction (`completeStaffJoin`, in the verify hook), so there is never a super-admin without two-step. An unfinished sign-up is deleted on the next staff-invitation action or refused sign-in, not by a job.
- **Staff are never members.** A staff account can't belong to, create or join an organization: onboarding, customer invitations (create, resend, preview, accept) and every `member` procedure refuse it (NOT_FOUND for procedures), and the database refuses a staff `members` row (`members_refuse_staff`) and a customer `platform_admins` row (`platform_admins_staff_only`). A customer invite to a staff email is a row problem; a customer sign-up with a staff email looks normal on screen and the inbox gets a staff note.
- **Their own console home and account.** Staff sign in on the normal sign-in page and land on the console Dashboard (`nextStep: 'console'`); any customer path sends them to `/console`. Customer menus, More and ⌘K have no console entry and the console has no "Back to {organization}". Their own sign-in is managed on My account in the console (`/console/account`), with the same personal procedures and cards as My security; two-step reads "Required for Vento Real Estate staff" and can't be turned off. A staff person's account events (sign-in, two-step, passkeys, console opened) go to the platform trail with channel `account`, since they have no organization trail; Activity and the Dashboard leave them out.
- **Reset and removal.** Another super-admin resets a lost two-step in the console (sessions end, passkeys and authenticator removed, a password link emailed, two-step set up again at the next sign-in); nobody resets their own. Removing a super-admin closes the staff account in the same transaction (sessions, passkeys, authenticator and password removed; name kept on history); inviting the same email again starts fresh on the same account row.
- **Commands.** `superadmin grant` refuses ("Super-admins join by invitation. Use superadmin invite."). `superadmin invite --break-glass` emails the link and never prints it. `superadmin invite --bootstrap` works only with no active super-admin and prints a one-time 48-hour link (actor "Vento Real Estate (setup)"); a re-run replaces the open bootstrap invitation. `superadmin reset-two-step --break-glass` (owner, 27 Nov 2026) clears a super-admin's two-step and emails them a password link (a password alone never gets back in); it is command-only in the parity registry, with the console's **Reset two-step** as its counterpart.
- **At deploy** (migration 0014), a super-admin who is also an active member of an organization stops being a super-admin (platform event, reason "Staff accounts are separate from customer accounts"), keeps their customer access, and re-joins by invitation with a different email. The remaining super-admins become staff accounts.
- **What this amends.** 0015: a super-admin is a staff account, never a role on a customer account. 0021: no console entry in customer menus, no "Back to {organization}", sign-in lands on the Dashboard, and the no-organization passkey limitation is replaced by My account.

## Consequences
- Staff power and customer data never meet in one account; the support view (slice 2) becomes the only way staff see a customer workspace.
- A staff member who wants to try the product as a customer needs a second email.
- An unfinished staff sign-up can linger until the next staff-invitation action or sign-in attempt.
- At deploy, the owner's own account may stop being a super-admin; the bootstrap invitation must be run straight after, and a second super-admin invited at once (RUNBOOK).
- In the console nobody can reset their own two-step. From the host, `superadmin reset-two-step --break-glass` may reset your own (every session ended, a password link emailed, two-step set up again before the console opens; the security inbox, the other super-admins and the person are emailed), so a lone super-admin who loses both phone and backup codes recovers through their inbox rather than being locked out for good. Production still keeps at least two super-admins.
- Turning a former staff email into a customer account needs a migration (the kind is fixed).

## Alternatives considered
- Keep super-admins as customer accounts with a role: what the owner asked to end; mixes powers and keeps the passkey gap.
- Derive "staff" from `platform_admins` alone: a removed super-admin or a half-finished sign-up would read as a customer, and could then join an organization with the same email.
- A separate identity store or Better Auth instance for staff: two sign-in systems, two cookies, and passkeys under a second relying party, for no gain at this team size.
- Staff self sign-up restricted to @ventoerp.com: no domain mailboxes yet (owner's decision 8, deferred).
- Granting super-admin when the password is set, with two-step later: a window in which a super-admin has no second factor.
