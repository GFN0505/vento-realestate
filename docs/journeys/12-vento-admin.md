# Vento admin

Journeys for Vento staff (super-admins), not for customers. They run in the **Vento console** (`/console`, decision 0021): a staff-only area on the web app's own origin, with its own shell and the amber label "Vento staff · all customers". **Trust model:** every change is made by a super-admin signed in with fresh two-step (a passkey, authenticator code or backup code proven in the last 5 minutes opens a console window that closes after 15 minutes without console activity or 8 hours), and is attributed to that person from the session, never typed. The `pnpm flags | plans | superadmin` change commands are **break-glass** only, for when the console or sign-in is down: they refuse without `--break-glass`, still need `--by` and `--reason`, and every break-glass change emails all other super-admins. See decisions 0015 and 0021 and `docs/RUNBOOK.md` §3.

Spec: [admin-console](../specs/admin-console.md) (builds on [feature-flags](../specs/feature-flags.md)). Plan: [admin-console](../plans/admin-console.md). Release plan: [release-plan](../release-plan.md).

Staff journeys have no customer help article: the console is documented here and in `docs/RUNBOOK.md` (see `docs/help/README.md`).

## J-ADM-01 · Release a feature, stage by stage
**Who / how often:** Vento super-admin (Ada), every release (every few weeks); the kill switch is rare, and may come from a phone.
**Starts from:** the feature being *releasable* (built, tested, reviewed, help article written, and the owner's hands-on review done; release plan section 6) and the owner deciding to release it, to everyone or to chosen organizations first. Steps 2–4 are optional: a feature can go straight from `internal` to `on`.

**Steps**
1. User menu → **Vento console** (or ⌘K "Open Vento console"). If Ada proved a passkey or code in the last 5 minutes (she just signed in), the console opens at once; otherwise *Confirm it's you (console)* asks for a passkey or a code. She lands on *Organizations*.
2. Once per pilot: *Organizations* → search "Lekki" → **Lekki Partners Ltd** → *Organization detail* ⋯ → **Change release group** → **Pilot** → **Review** → the preview names the features Lekki gains → Reason "Pilot agreement signed" → **Move to pilot**. Toast "Lekki Partners Ltd is now in the pilot group. Takes effect on its next request."
3. **Releases** → *Releases list* (stage per feature, holders, plans that include it) → **Sales** (1) → *Feature detail*: stage, prerequisites, plans, organizations that have it by group, live overrides, recent changes.
4. **Stage: Pilot** (2) → *Stage confirm* "Move Sales to pilot?" with the preview: "Sales will be available to 3 organizations (1 internal, 2 pilot)." plus any in-no-plan warning → Reason "Release 1 pilot" → **Move Sales to pilot** (3). Toast "Sales is now at pilot. Takes effect within 30 seconds."
5. A pilot member who can view Sales reloads or changes page: **Sales** appears in their sidebar with "Early access".
6. At release: **Stage: On** → confirm → reason → **Move Sales to on**. The "Early access" tag disappears; standard organizations whose plan includes Sales now see it.
7. If something breaks (kill switch, from a phone): console → **Releases** (1) → **Sales** (2) → **Turn off for everyone** (3, always at the foot of the page, never in a menu) → "Turn off Sales for everyone? It stops for all 41 organizations, including overrides and plans. Nothing is deleted." → Reason "Receipt allocation bug" → **Turn off Sales** (4). The feature detail then shows "Off for everyone since 26 Nov 2026, 21:14 · Ada · Receipt allocation bug".
8. **Break-glass** (console or sign-in down): `pnpm flags stage sales off --break-glass --reason "Receipt allocation bug" --by ada@ventoerp.com` → the same preview lines → `y`. Without `--break-glass` the command refuses: "Make this change in the Vento console. If the console is down, add --break-glass."

**Behind the scenes:** each change calls the same platform operation as the command, in one transaction: one platform audit event (who from the session, channel `console`, operator "console · Chrome on Mac · 102.89.34.x", before → after, reason) and, for release group and override changes, a plain-words entry in that organization's audit trail ("Vento turned on Estates for your organization"; actor "Vento", never the staff name or reason). The confirm carries the before-state it was previewed against and an idempotency key, so a retry never applies twice. API servers refresh their snapshot within 10 seconds; web apps refetch on the next page change or tab focus. Paystack webhooks keep recording money whatever the stage. A break-glass change is tagged `command` and emails every other active super-admin ("Moved Sales to off from the command line": who, the host, the time and the reason).
**Done when:** the feature is available to exactly the organizations its stage, groups, plans and overrides say, and *Activity* shows the change with who, why and **Console** (or the rose **Command line** tag).
**Watch for:** two super-admins changing the same feature at once (the second is refused: "Sales moved to pilot a moment ago (by Ada). Check the preview and try again.", and the page refreshes); the console window closing mid-change (*Confirm it's you* opens over the dialog, the reason is kept, and the change runs after the proof); a dropped connection while confirming ("Couldn't reach Vento. Your change wasn't made. Try again.", reason kept); moving to the stage it's already at (refused); a reason under 3 characters (button stays disabled, "Add a reason so the activity says why."); a prerequisite unavailable to an organization ("Buyer portal needs Sales and Finance…"); moving to `on` a feature that no plan includes (warning); lowering a stage hides data from organizations that used it (danger line with record count; nothing deleted); keys in data the code no longer has (banner on *Releases list*); a member mid-task when it goes off (toast, back to Home); a person in an internal and a standard organization never sees internal-only features in the standard one.
**Target:** 3 clicks from *Releases list* for a stage change; kill switch 4 taps from the console on a phone, under 30 seconds; effective within 30 seconds. **Test:** `apps/web/e2e/console/releases.spec.ts` (console stage change, kill switch on mobile, stale conflict), `apps/web/e2e/shell/feature-availability.spec.ts` (navigation, 404, no flash); API: `apps/api/src/platform/console-parity.test.ts` (every change command has a console procedure writing the same audit rows; refusal without `--break-glass`; break-glass email), `apps/api/src/platform/flags-cli.test.ts` (commands) **Status:** built (console, slice 1, on `feat/admin-console`; e2e spec being written)

## J-ADM-02 · Put an organization on a plan, start a trial, record a deal
**Who / how often:** Vento super-admin (Ada or Tunde), when a customer signs, upgrades, trials or negotiates, or asks "why can't my team see X?"; weekly once selling.
**Starts from:** a signed order, a trial request, a pilot agreement, or a customer question.

**Steps**
1. *Organizations* (console home): "214 organizations · 3,912 people", stat cards **Organizations**, **On trial**, **Pilot**, **Over a limit** (each filters the list), chips, and search by organization name or owner email. Search "Lekki" → **Lekki Partners Ltd** (1).
2. *Organization detail*: header ("Starter v1 · Standard · owner Bisi Lawal (bisi@lekkipartners.ng) · customer since …"), Plan card with limits and use, Trial, Add-ons, Overrides, Features and Activity cards.
3. **Why doesn't Lekki have Commissions?** Features card → filter **Not available** (2): "Commissions · Not in Starter; in Growth and Enterprise".
4. **Change plan** (2) → *Change plan sheet*, current plan preselected, latest version ("Use an older version" under More options) → **Growth** (3) → **Review** (4) → *Plan confirm* "Move Lekki Partners Ltd to Growth?" with over-limit, features gained and lost, and records that drop out of view → Reason "Signed annual Growth" → **Move to Growth** (5). Toast "Lekki Partners Ltd is now on Growth (version 2). Takes effect on its next request."
5. Trial: ⋯ (1) → **Start trial** (2), next plan up and 30 days preselected → **Review** (3) → "Start a 30-day Enterprise trial?" with the end date and "Owners get an email 7 days and 1 day before it ends" → reason → **Start 30-day trial** (4). The Trial card then shows "Enterprise trial · ends 26 Dec 2026 (30 days)" with **End trial**.
6. Deal: ⋯ → **Add override** → Plan · Projects limit · 8 (until empty = no end date) → **Review** → reason "Negotiated: 8 projects" → **Set projects limit to 8**. From a Features row, ⋯ **Release override** or **Plan override** opens the same sheet prefilled.
7. AI: ⋯ → **Add AI** (₦150,000 a month prefilled) → **Review** → "Owners can then switch AI features on in Settings → AI" → reason → **Add AI**. Later ⋯ → **Change AI budget** → ₦250,000 → **Change to ₦250,000** in one step (no remove and re-add). The owner then sees *Settings → AI* (J-AI-04).
8. A newer plan version exists: the Plan card says "Growth v3 is available" with **Move to v3** (the same change-plan sheet).
9. The owner sees in their audit trail "Vento moved your organization to Growth", "Vento started a 30-day Enterprise trial (ends 26 Dec 2026)", "Vento changed your AI budget to ₦250,000 a month".
10. **Publish a new plan version:** **Plans** → **Growth** → *Plan detail* (versions side by side) → **Publish new version** → *Publish sheet* step 1: features grouped by module starting from the latest version, limits with "Unlimited" → **Review** → "2 features added, 1 removed. 47 organizations stay on Growth v2 until you move them." → reason → **Publish Growth v3**. Nobody moves; organizations on v2 show "Growth v3 is available". A new plan uses the same sheet with key and name.
11. **Break-glass:** the same changes as `pnpm plans assign | trial | trial --end | addon | addon --remove | override … | publish` with `--break-glass --reason … --by …` (changing the AI budget has no command: remove and add).

**Behind the scenes:** platform and organization audit events in the same transaction as the change (the organization's entry says "Vento", never the staff name or reason); API servers refresh within 10 seconds. A trial ends by itself at its end date (no job needed); a daily job (`plans notify-trials`, channel `scheduled`) emails owners 7 days and 1 day before, and Home shows the trial line to owners in the last 7 days. When it ends, its features disappear, data stays, and creates over the restored limits are refused. Changing the AI budget ends the current add-on row and starts a new one, so the Add-ons card's "since" is the current budget's start. The console shows facts only: owner name and email, counts and dates, never members' names, buyers, money, documents or KYC.
**Done when:** the organization's available features and limits match its plan, add-ons and overrides, and the Features card's reasons agree with them.
**Watch for:** an organization over a limit after a downgrade (amber at the limit, rose over it: "7 of 5 · over the limit. Nothing is archived; new projects are refused."; listed under **Over a limit**); an organization with no plan ("No plan" in amber with **Choose a plan**); an internal organization ("Internal: every feature, no limits"); starting a trial when one is running ("Lekki Partners Ltd already has a trial ending 2 Jan 2027." with an **End trial** link); adding AI when it's already there (refused with a link to **Change AI budget**); an override "until" in the past, a non-number limit, a budget of ₦0, or a trial of 0 or over 90 days (field says what's needed, nothing sent); clearing an override says what applies after ("Projects limit goes back to Growth's 5"); two creates racing for the last slot (only one succeeds); members and pending invitations both count towards `members`; archived records don't count; a non-owner hitting a limit is told whom to ask, never the plan or price; Starter organization owner searching ⌘K for "commissions" ("Available on Growth" with **Talk to Vento**), non-owners see nothing; no organizations match ("No organizations match these filters." with **Clear filters**).
**Target:** "why doesn't Lekki have X?" in 2 clicks; plan change 5 clicks from *Organizations* (the spec's 4 plus the sheet's **Review**); trial 4 clicks from *Organization detail*; AI budget change one step. **Test:** `apps/web/e2e/console/organizations.spec.ts` (search, Features "why", change plan with over-limit line, start trial, add override, AI budget in one step, owner's audit entry), `apps/web/e2e/console/plans.spec.ts` (publish a new version, review diff, "is available"); API: `apps/api/src/platform/console-parity.test.ts`, `apps/api/src/platform/explain.test.ts` (Features reasons agree with availability), `apps/api/src/platform/plans-cli.test.ts`, `apps/api/src/platform/limits.test.ts`, `apps/api/src/platform/limits.concurrency.test.ts`; `e2e/settings/plan-limits.spec.ts` **Status:** built (console, slice 1, on `feat/admin-console`; e2e specs being written)

## J-ADM-03 · Manage super-admins and review platform activity
**Who / how often:** Vento super-admin; adding or removing one is rare (a new hire, a leaver); reviewing activity weekly, and at once after a break-glass email.
**Starts from:** a new staff member who needs the console (with a Vento account and two-step on), someone leaving, or a "Moved … from the command line" email.

**Steps**
1. **Super-admins** → *Super-admins*: each person with email, since, granted by and last console use; "No second approver is needed while there are fewer than 4 super-admins."
2. **Add super-admin** (1) → *Add super-admin sheet*: email "tunde@ventoerp.com" → **Review** (2) → "Make Tunde a super-admin?" (on the fourth: "This is the fourth super-admin. The owner asked for a second approver from now on; that's not built yet.") → Reason "Customer success lead" → **Make Tunde a super-admin** (3). Toast "Tunde Bello is now a Vento super-admin."
3. Tunde gets "You're now a Vento super-admin" ("Open the console from your user menu. You'll need your passkey or authenticator code each time."); every other super-admin gets a short notice. Tunde now sees **Vento console** in his user menu and ⌘K; on My security his two-step shows "Required because you're a Vento super-admin" with no **Turn off**. If he belongs to no organization, he lands on the console after sign-in.
4. Removing: **Remove** on Tunde's row → "Remove Tunde Bello as a super-admin? They lose the Vento console at once. Their own organizations aren't affected." → reason → **Remove Tunde**. There's no **Remove** on your own row.
5. **Activity** → *Activity*: Vento's platform audit trail, newest first: when, who, what in words ("Moved Sales to pilot"), organization or feature, before → after, reason, and a **Console** tag, a rose **Command line** tag, or **Scheduled** (the daily trial job). Filters: person, feature, kind and dates, all kept in the address; an organization's **See all** opens Activity filtered to it ("Show all organizations" clears it).
6. After a break-glass email: filter by the person named → the **Command line** row shows the host, the change and the reason.

**Behind the scenes:** adding and removing call the same operations as `pnpm superadmin grant | revoke` (break-glass), each writing one platform audit event. Removal closes the person's console window on all their sessions in the same transaction; their customer sessions carry on. Emails go after the change commits. Opening the console is recorded in the person's own account trail (`console.opened`), not on *Activity*, which lists changes only. Rows from before the console carry no channel and are labelled **Command line** or **Scheduled** at read time.
**Done when:** the right people are super-admins, every one has two-step on, and every platform change in *Activity* has a named person, a channel and a reason.
**Watch for:** an email with no Vento account ("No Vento account uses tunde@ventoerp.com."); an account without two-step ("Tunde Bello needs two-step sign-in on before becoming a super-admin."); someone who already is one (refused); removing yourself (no button; the API refuses "You can't remove yourself. Ask another super-admin."); a removed super-admin's next console request answers *not found* and `/console` shows 404; a super-admin whose two-step an owner reset sees "Set up two-step to use the Vento console." with **Set up two-step** until they set the app up again; no activity matching the filters (filtered-empty with clear).
**Target:** add a super-admin in 3 clicks; find a person's changes in 2. **Test:** `apps/web/e2e/console/super-admins.spec.ts` (add, no Remove on own row, remove, Activity by person with Console and Command line tags), `apps/web/e2e/console/access.spec.ts` (404 and no console code for customers and revoked super-admins, fresh proof, *Confirm it's you* by code, backup code and passkey, mid-task timeout, no-organization landing, two-step reset); API: `apps/api/src/platform/console-access.test.ts` (access walk and console window), `apps/api/src/access/console-window.test.ts` **Status:** built (console, slice 1, on `feat/admin-console`; e2e specs being written)

## Settled by the owner (26 Nov 2026)
- Pilots are optional; if used, they're on Enterprise at no charge while piloting.
- Trial reminders go to owners 7 days and 1 day before the end.
- Changes take effect within 30 seconds.
- No second approver for flag and plan changes while fewer than four people are super-admins.
- The console lives at **`/console`** on the web app's origin (`app.ventoerp.com/console`); 404 for everyone who isn't an active super-admin.
- Entering needs a passkey or code from the last **5 minutes**; the window closes after **15 minutes** without console activity or **8 hours**.
- A trial defaults to **30 days**, at most **90**; AI is prefilled at **₦150,000 a month**; a reason needs at least **3 characters**.
- Change commands need **`--break-glass`**, and every break-glass change emails all other super-admins.
- Any existing account with two-step on can be made a super-admin; restricting to `@ventoerp.com` addresses waits for domain mailboxes.

## Assumptions to confirm
- Plan change is 5 clicks, not the spec's 4: every change sheet has a **Review** step between its fields and the confirm, so the preview is computed from the final choice. Review-only changes (stage, kill switch, end trial, remove AI, clear override, remove super-admin) preview at once and keep their targets.
- *Activity* has no organization drop-down: an organization filter comes from its **See all** link (the spec listed organization among the filters).
- Super-admins' **Remove** is a button on the row, not in a ⋯ menu.
