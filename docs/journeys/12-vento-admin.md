# Vento Real Estate admin

Journeys for Vento Real Estate staff (super-admins), not for customers. They run in the **Vento Real Estate console** (`realestate.ventoerp.com/console`, decision 0021): a staff-only area on the web app's own origin, with its own shell and the amber label "Vento Real Estate staff · all customers". **Since slice 1b (27 Nov 2026)** super-admins have **staff accounts**: they join only by a staff invitation, are never members of any organization, sign in on the normal sign-in page and land on the console **Dashboard**, and manage their own sign-in on **My account** in the console. **Trust model:** every change is made by a super-admin signed in with fresh two-step (a passkey, authenticator code or backup code proven in the last 5 minutes opens a console window that closes after 15 minutes without console activity or 8 hours), and is attributed to that person from the session, never typed. The `pnpm flags | plans | superadmin` change commands are **break-glass** only, for when the console or sign-in is down: they refuse without `--break-glass`, still need `--by` and `--reason`, and every break-glass change emails all other super-admins. See decisions 0015, 0021, 0022 and 0023 and `docs/RUNBOOK.md` §3 and §4.

Spec: [admin-console](../specs/admin-console.md) (builds on [feature-flags](../specs/feature-flags.md)). Plan: [admin-console](../plans/admin-console.md). Release plan: [release-plan](../release-plan.md).

Staff journeys have no customer help article: the console is documented here and in `docs/RUNBOOK.md` (see `docs/help/README.md`).

## J-ADM-01 · Release a feature, stage by stage
**Who / how often:** super-admin (Ada), every release (every few weeks); the kill switch is rare, and may come from a phone.
**Starts from:** the feature being *releasable* (built, tested, reviewed, help article written, and the owner's hands-on review done; release plan section 6) and the owner deciding to release it, to everyone or to chosen organizations first. Steps 2–4 are optional: a feature can go straight from `internal` to `on`.

**Steps**
1. Ada signs in (password and code, or a passkey) and lands on the console *Dashboard* (1b; slice 1 opened the console from the user menu and landed on *Organizations*). If her console window has closed since, *Confirm it's you (console)* asks for a passkey or a code.
2. Once per pilot: *Organizations* → search "Lekki" → **Lekki Partners Ltd** → *Organization detail* ⋯ → **Change release group** → **Pilot** → **Review** → the preview names the features Lekki gains → Reason "Pilot agreement signed" → **Move to pilot**. Toast "Lekki Partners Ltd is now in the pilot group. Takes effect on its next request."
3. **Releases** → *Releases list* (stage per feature, holders, plans that include it) → **Sales** (1) → *Feature detail*: stage, prerequisites, plans, organizations that have it by group, live overrides, recent changes.
4. **Stage: Pilot** (2) → *Stage confirm* "Move Sales to pilot?" with the preview: "Sales will be available to 3 organizations (1 internal, 2 pilot)." plus any in-no-plan warning → Reason "Release 1 pilot" → **Move Sales to pilot** (3). Toast "Sales is now at pilot. Takes effect within 30 seconds."
5. A pilot member who can view Sales reloads or changes page: **Sales** appears in their sidebar with "Early access".
6. At release: **Stage: On** → confirm → reason → **Move Sales to on**. The "Early access" tag disappears; standard organizations whose plan includes Sales now see it.
7. If something breaks (kill switch, from a phone): console → **Releases** (1) → **Sales** (2) → **Turn off for everyone** (3, always at the foot of the page, never in a menu) → "Turn off Sales for everyone? It stops for all 41 organizations, including overrides and plans. Nothing is deleted." → Reason "Receipt allocation bug" → **Turn off Sales** (4). The feature detail then shows "Off for everyone since 26 Nov 2026, 21:14 · Ada · Receipt allocation bug".
8. **Break-glass** (console or sign-in down): `pnpm flags stage sales off --break-glass --reason "Receipt allocation bug" --by ada@ventoerp.com` → the same preview lines → `y`. Without `--break-glass` the command refuses: "Make this change in the Vento Real Estate console. If the console is down, add --break-glass."

**Behind the scenes:** each change calls the same platform operation as the command, in one transaction: one platform audit event (who from the session, channel `console`, operator "console · Chrome on Mac · 102.89.34.x", before → after, reason) and, for release group and override changes, a plain-words entry in that organization's audit trail ("Vento Real Estate turned on Estates for your organization"; actor "Vento Real Estate", never the staff name or reason). The confirm carries the before-state it was previewed against and an idempotency key, so a retry never applies twice. API servers refresh their snapshot within 10 seconds; web apps refetch on the next page change or tab focus. Paystack webhooks keep recording money whatever the stage. A break-glass change is tagged `command` and emails every other active super-admin ("Moved Sales to off from the command line": who, the host, the time and the reason).
**Done when:** the feature is available to exactly the organizations its stage, groups, plans and overrides say, and *Activity* shows the change with who, why and **Console** (or the rose **Command line** tag).
**Watch for:** two super-admins changing the same feature at once (the second is refused: "Sales moved to pilot a moment ago (by Ada). Check the preview and try again.", and the page refreshes); the console window closing mid-change (*Confirm it's you* opens over the dialog, the reason is kept, and the change runs after the proof); a dropped connection while confirming ("Couldn't reach Vento Real Estate. Your change wasn't made. Try again.", reason kept); moving to the stage it's already at (refused); a reason under 3 characters (button stays disabled, "Add a reason so the activity says why."); a prerequisite unavailable to an organization ("Buyer portal needs Sales and Finance…"); moving to `on` a feature that no plan includes (warning); lowering a stage hides data from organizations that used it (danger line with record count; nothing deleted); keys in data the code no longer has (banner on *Releases list*); a member mid-task when it goes off (toast, back to Home); a person in an internal and a standard organization never sees internal-only features in the standard one.
**Target:** 3 clicks from *Releases list* for a stage change; kill switch 4 taps from the console on a phone, under 30 seconds; effective within 30 seconds. **Test:** `apps/web/e2e/console/releases.spec.ts` (console stage change, kill switch on mobile, stale conflict), `apps/web/e2e/shell/feature-availability.spec.ts` (navigation, 404, no flash); API: `apps/api/src/platform/console-parity.test.ts` (every change command has a console procedure writing the same audit rows; refusal without `--break-glass`; break-glass email), `apps/api/src/platform/flags-cli.test.ts` (commands) **Status:** built (console, slice 1, on `feat/admin-console`; e2e spec being written)

## J-ADM-03 · Manage super-admins and review platform activity
**Who / how often:** super-admin; inviting or removing one is rare (a new hire, a leaver); reviewing activity weekly, and at once after a break-glass email.
**Starts from:** a new staff member who needs the console (no account needed; 1b), someone leaving, a colleague who lost their phone, or a "Moved … from the command line" email.

**Steps**
1. **Super-admins** → *Super-admins*: each person with email, since, invited by and last console use; "No second approver is needed while there are fewer than 4 super-admins." An *Invitations* section lists open and expired staff invitations.
2. **Invite super-admin** (1) → *Invite super-admin sheet*: email "tunde@ventoerp.com", name "Tunde Bello", reason "Customer success lead" → **Review** (2) → "Invite Tunde Bello to be a super-admin?" with "Tunde Bello (tunde@ventoerp.com) gets an email to join. The link works for 48 hours." and "They become a super-admin only after setting a password and two-step sign-in." (on the fourth, active plus invited: "This would be the fourth super-admin…") → **Send invitation** (3). Toast "Invitation sent to Tunde Bello".
3. Tunde gets "Ada Okafor invited you to the Vento Real Estate console"; every other super-admin gets a notice. He joins through J-ADM-05; when he finishes, the others get "Tunde Bello joined as a Vento Real Estate super-admin" and his row moves from *Invitations* to the list.
4. **Resend** (new 48-hour link, once a minute) or **Withdraw** (confirm with reason; the link stops at once) from the *Invitations* section.
5. Lost phone: ⋯ **Reset two-step** on Tunde's row → "Reset Tunde Bello's two-step? They're signed out everywhere, their passkeys are removed, and they get an email to set a new password. They set up two-step again at their next sign-in." → reason → **Reset two-step**. Toast "Tunde Bello's two-step was reset". (Shown only on another person's row whose two-step is on.) With the console down: `pnpm superadmin reset-two-step tunde@ventoerp.com --break-glass --by ada@ventoerp.com --reason "Lost phone"`, which keeps Tunde's password (no reset link) and emails him a security alert and the others the break-glass email.
6. Removing: ⋯ **Remove** on Tunde's row → "Remove Tunde Bello as a super-admin? Their staff account closes: they're signed out everywhere and can't sign in. Their name stays on past activity." → reason → **Remove Tunde**. Neither action is on your own row.
7. **Activity** → *Activity*: Vento Real Estate's platform audit trail (1b adds invited, resent, withdrawn, joined and two-step reset), newest first: when, who, what in words ("Moved Sales to pilot"), organization or feature, before → after, reason, and a **Console** tag, a rose **Command line** tag, or **Scheduled** (the daily trial job). Filters: person, feature, kind and dates, all kept in the address; an organization's **See all** opens Activity filtered to it ("Show all organizations" clears it).
8. After a break-glass email (or its row in the Dashboard's *Needs attention*): filter by the person named → the **Command line** row shows the host, the change and the reason.

**Behind the scenes:** inviting and removing call the same operations as `pnpm superadmin invite | revoke` (break-glass; `grant` is retired in 1b and refuses "Super-admins join by invitation. Use superadmin invite."), each writing one platform audit event (`superadmin.invited`, `superadmin.invitation_resent`, `superadmin.invitation_withdrawn`, `superadmin.joined`, `superadmin.two_step_reset`, and the existing `superadmin.revoked`). Removal closes the staff account: every session ends and the password, passkeys and authenticator are removed, in the same transaction. A console reset ends sessions, removes passkeys and authenticator and emails a 30-minute password link; the person's account event `two_step.reset` goes to the platform trail. Every invite, resend, withdraw, join, reset and removal takes the same lock, so counts (5 open, one active super-admin left) are serial. Emails go after the change commits; break-glass invitations are emailed, never printed. Opening the console is recorded in the person's own account trail (`console.opened`), not on *Activity*, which lists changes only. Rows from before the console carry no channel and are labelled **Command line** or **Scheduled** at read time.
**Done when:** the right people are super-admins, every one has two-step on, and every platform change in *Activity* has a named person, a channel and a reason.
**Watch for:** an email with a customer account ("tunde@gmail.com already has a Vento Real Estate customer account. Invite a different email, such as their @ventoerp.com address."); someone who already is a super-admin or already has an open invitation (refused, with **Resend**); a sixth open invitation ("There are 5 open invitations…"); resending twice in a minute; an expired invitation (row **Expired**, resend or withdraw); removing yourself or resetting your own two-step (no action; the API refuses "You can't remove yourself. Ask another super-admin." / "You can't reset your own two-step. Ask another super-admin."); a removed super-admin can't sign in (the same answer as a wrong password) and `/console` shows 404; removing the last super-admin is refused ("There must always be at least one super-admin."); a lone super-admin who lost phone and backup codes can't be reset by anyone (keep two); re-inviting a removed person's email starts fresh; a super-admin whose two-step was reset sets it up again before the console opens; making new backup codes or adding a passkey needs a code or passkey from the last 5 minutes; no activity matching the filters (filtered-empty with clear).
**Target:** invite a super-admin in 3 clicks; find a person's changes in 2. **Test:** `apps/web/e2e/console/super-admins.spec.ts` (invite in 3 clicks, Invitations row with expiry, resend, withdraw then the link shows withdrawn, a customer email refused, reset two-step on another super-admin, remove then that person is signed out and can't sign in, no actions on own row, Activity by person with Console and Command line tags), `apps/web/e2e/console/access.spec.ts` (staff sign-in lands on the Dashboard; `/` and `/settings/team` send staff to `/console`; 404 and no console code for customers and revoked super-admins; no console entry in the customer user menu, More or ⌘K; fresh proof, *Confirm it's you* by code, backup code and passkey, mid-task timeout, two-step reset); API: `apps/api/src/platform/console-access.test.ts` (access walk, staff walk and console window), `apps/api/src/access/console-window.test.ts`, `apps/api/src/platform/staff-accounts.test.ts` (closing, discarding unfinished sign-ups), `apps/api/src/platform/staff-rules.test.ts` (staff refused by onboarding and customer invitations), `apps/api/src/db/staff-accounts.migration.test.ts` (the deploy rule and the three triggers), `apps/api/src/platform/superadmin-cli.test.ts` (`invite`, `--bootstrap`, `grant` refusal, `reset-two-step --break-glass`), `apps/api/src/platform/console-parity.test.ts` **Status:** built (1b on `feat/admin-console`, PR #9, awaiting review; the updated e2e specs are being written)
